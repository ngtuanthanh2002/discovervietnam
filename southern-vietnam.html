Southern Vietnam

Southern Vietnam is known for its tropical landscapes, rivers, fruit gardens, and unique Mekong Delta culture. Visitors can experience a different side of Vietnam through its waterways and local communities.

Can Tho

Can Tho is one of the main destinations in the Mekong Delta. It is known for the Cai Rang Floating Market and its network of rivers and waterways.

Ben Tre

Ben Tre is known for its coconut gardens, fruit orchards, boat rides, and peaceful countryside.
