Travel Tips & Contact

Planning a trip to Vietnam? This page provides useful information to help visitors prepare for their trip.

  • Visa Requirements

    Visitors should check the current visa requirements before traveling to Vietnam. Requirements may vary depending on nationality.

  • Currency

    The official currency of Vietnam is the Vietnamese Dong (VND).

  • Weather

    Vietnam has different weather conditions across the North, Central, and South. Visitors should check the weather before planning their trip.
